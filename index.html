Why CAB

More than a gift.
A year of care.

When you're abroad, you send money. But money disappears — into groceries, bills, things that need fixing. Rarely into something just for them. CAB exists because the people who matter most to you almost never spend on themselves.

"They won't buy it for themselves. But they'll accept the gift of being cared for — if it comes from you."

CAB is a gifting platform built for Nepali expats — for those who grew up far from where they are now, and who carry the people back home with them. Whether you're gifting your parents, your grandparents, a sibling, or anyone who holds a special place in your life, CAB gives them a full year of wellness experiences.

We take care of everything — booking, coordination, pickup, drop-off. The gift arrives on a date that matters to you. The massages are redeemable whenever they choose. The retreats are moments they'll look forward to. You'll know they're cared for.

It spreads throughout the year

Unlike a one-time gesture, a CAB plan gives your loved one recurring moments of joy — massages they can book any time, a hamper arriving on a special day, retreats to look forward to. The care keeps coming, long after the gift is given.

They won't spend on themselves

Money sent home is absorbed into daily life. A gifted wellness plan guarantees it's spent on the person — not on bills, not on the household. On them.

Community, connection, and belonging

Loneliness and isolation are some of the most invisible health challenges. Our retreats and wellness days bring people together — building friendships, shared experience, and a genuine sense of community.

We remove every friction point

Pickup and drop is included in every plan. We book, coordinate, and follow up. Your loved one simply shows up — and is taken care of. That's the full promise of CAB.

The Experience

How it works

CAB is designed to feel personal from the very first moment — for both the person gifting, and the person receiving. Here is exactly what happens after you purchase.

1

You Choose a Plan & Fill a Form

After purchasing, you fill a short form — who the gift is for, their location in Nepal, your chosen delivery date, and the personal note you'd like on their card.

2

The Gift Reveal

On the date you chose, three things happen: our team calls your loved one to announce the gift warmly and personally, a beautifully presented Wellness Hamper arrives at their door with your handwritten card enclosed, and we send them a WhatsApp message with a personalised gift image — so the moment lands completely.

3

We Guide Them All Year

Our team follows up proactively — helping them book massages, coordinating pickups and drops, gathering health information before retreats, and gently reminding them to use what's theirs. Nothing expires without a fight from us.

4

You Stay Connected

After each experience — a massage, a retreat, a consultation — we send you a photo or update. At year end, you receive a summary of everything your loved one experienced. You gave them care. We make sure you feel it too.

5

The Year Renews

As the year closes, we reach out to you first — before anyone else — to renew. Founding families are offered renewal at their locked pilot pricing. The care continues, for as long as you want it to.

One-on-one, all year. CAB is not a booking platform. It is a personal care service. Every family has a dedicated point of contact who knows their story, follows up proactively, and ensures nothing falls through the cracks. You don't need to coordinate — we do.

A Note from the Founder

Why I built CAB

As I've grown older, I've come to understand something that took me years to see clearly: wellness — the kind that reaches your mind, your body, and the quiet spaces in between — is not a luxury. It is what it means to live well. And yet, for most of our parents and grandparents in Nepal, it has always felt like something reserved for someone else. Something foreign. Something they simply don't do for themselves.

My parents, until recently, had never spent on holistic wellness. Not because they didn't deserve it — but because the culture of it hadn't reached them. They spent their lives giving everything to their families, their homes, their children. And somehow, in all that giving, the habit of caring for themselves simply never formed.

I also noticed something else: as they got older, their world got smaller. The active community they once had — the friendships, the shared purpose, the sense of belonging — slowly faded into the rhythm of home and chores. That kind of quiet isolation is one of the most invisible forms of suffering, and we rarely talk about it.

I found, oddly, that it was easier to gift wellness to others than to give it to myself. And I thought — if that's how it works, then let gifting be the door through which this culture of care finally enters.

CAB is my attempt to answer the question I kept asking myself: what can I give them that actually contributes to their life — not just fills a day, not just arrives in a packet, but genuinely changes how they feel and how they spend their time?

I am 23. I don't have a large team or a big office. What I have is a deep belief that the people who raised us deserve to be cared for — and a commitment to making that possible, one family at a time.

— Shreha, Founder, CAB

Common Questions

Before you reach out

Everything you might want to know — answered honestly.

What if my loved one is unwell on a retreat day? +

We understand that health doesn't follow a schedule. If your loved one is unwell on a retreat date, please reach out to us as soon as possible. We will work with you to explore the best option — whether that is carrying their retreat forward to the following year's programme, or arranging alternative wellness services of equivalent value at a time that suits them. We are always on your side.

What if they are hesitant or don't want to attend? +

This is more common than you might think — and it's exactly why we handle the relationship with your loved one personally. Our team will gently introduce the plan, explain what the experience looks and feels like, and give them the time they need to warm to the idea. Many people just need to understand what they're walking into. We are patient, warm, and persistent — in the kindest possible way. That said, if they ultimately choose not to attend a retreat, we are unable to offer a refund for that session, as venues and experts are confirmed in advance.

How do I pay? +

We accept payment via Visa, Mastercard, and major debit cards. International bank transfers and Wise are also accepted for those sending from abroad. Once you reach out on WhatsApp or email, we will send you a secure payment link along with a confirmation of your chosen plan. Payment is required in full to secure your spot — particularly important during the pilot, where only 30 places are available.

When do the massages expire? +

All massages are valid for the duration of the plan year — 12 months from the date of purchase. We do not want any session to go unused, which is why our team follows up regularly throughout the year to help your loved one book their appointments at a time that suits them. We will always chase them gently before the year closes. Think of us as the reminder they didn't know they needed.

Can I upgrade to a higher plan later? +

Mid-year upgrades are not available as a standard option during the pilot — we want to ensure every experience is planned and delivered properly from the start. That said, we never want to say a flat no. If you feel strongly about upgrading, please message us and we will do our best to find a way. We are a small, founder-led team and we will always try.

What happens when the plan year ends? +

As your plan year draws to a close, we will reach out to offer renewal — before we open renewal to anyone else. Founding families are offered the opportunity to renew at their original pilot pricing, locked in for as long as they continue with CAB. The care does not have to stop. In fact, we hope it becomes something your family looks forward to every year.

Is CAB only for Nepali families abroad? +

CAB was built with Nepali expats in mind — people who understand the distance, the guilt, the love, and the culture. But the gift of wellness has no border. Anyone who has someone they care about in Nepal, and wants to give them something that genuinely contributes to their life, is welcome here.
